Dearest Vini,

✨ "To the friend whose warmth outshines a thousand festive lamps—may this Diwali bring magic to every corner of your world, Vini!"

Flipping through the chapters of life, some people are like gentle Diyas—they don’t just shine for themselves, they quietly make the world around them warmer, brighter, and infinitely more beautiful. You are that rare light, Vini.

As thousands of lamps flicker in the Diwali night sky, I wanted to craft this little palace of light to remind you how deeply appreciated and cherished you are. Thank you for the effortless grace, the genuine laughter, and the wonderful positivity you bring into every moment.

"हर दिया आपकी मुस्कुराहट सा जगमगाए,

कि आपका हर सपना सोने सा सच हो जाए!" ✨

May this Festival of Lights mark the beginning of your happiest, healthiest, and most triumphant chapter yet. Keep shining your unstoppable light!

With Warmest Wishes & Gratitude,

Your Well‑Wisher Forever 🪔✨
